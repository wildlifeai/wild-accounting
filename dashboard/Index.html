The cards total the years, projects, funding sources and statuses chosen. The chart is runway: income minus spend, cumulative, actuals to last month and budget from this month on. With every project and source shown, grouped by status, the lines start from the reserves on the Cockpit Settings sheet, so Secured only is the organisation's actual runway; any narrower view is funded money only. It follows Show and Filter, not the year. Each line's label says the month it goes short, or that it stays above zero to the last budgeted month, which only means the budgets stop there. Grouped by project, funding source or milestone, each line is one group's expected case: applications at their probability, or secured money only when Proposed is unticked.
